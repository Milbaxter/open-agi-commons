## Historical photograph

- Image: spacecraft communicators at Apollo 11 Mission Control.
- Date: 20 July 1969.
- People, left to right: Charles Duke, James Lovell, and Fred Haise.
- NASA image ID: S69-39601.
- Credit: NASA.
- [Primary image record](https://www.nasa.gov/image-article/spacecraft-communicators-mission-control/).
- [NASA archive gallery](https://www.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/static/history/ap11ann/kippsphotos/apollo.html).
